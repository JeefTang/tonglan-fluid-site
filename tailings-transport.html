Application · mining & coal

Tailings and coal slime pumps where wear life is the economics

High density, fine solids, continuous duty. The cost of a tailings pump is not the purchase price — it is the rebuilds, the downtime and the electricity.

Why centrifugal units fail here

A centrifugal slurry pump works by accelerating the slurry to impeller tip speed. At 30%+ solids, that velocity is exactly what erodes the impeller, volute and liner. Wear life of 15 days to 6 months is normal, and every rebuild costs production time.

What the V5 does differently

The five-plunger design moves slurry at low velocity, in discrete volumes. Abrasion is limited to the plunger surface, valve seats and seals — all consumables with published service intervals.

  • Ceramic plungers for abrasive solids
  • Alloy valve seats to hold volumetric efficiency
  • Wear parts 4,000 h+, overhaul 30,000 h+
  • Mechanical drive — no hydraulic oil circuit to maintain

Efficiency: above 83% against 25–50% for a worn centrifugal slurry pump. On a 250 m³/h duty at 120 m head, that difference is the difference between a manageable power bill and an unacceptable one.
