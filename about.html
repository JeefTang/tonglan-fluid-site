About us

An engineering team that started with the pump, not the catalogue

DALIAN HONGSHI TECHNOLOGY supplies high-pressure reciprocating plunger pumps and slurry pumps worldwide, with export operations handled from Dalian, Liaoning, China.

Background

The company was founded in 2018 in Pudong, Shanghai, as part of the Tongji Nanhui Science Park group. Product development draws on the Sichuan Machinery Research & Design Institute (established 1957), a state-owned engineering institute with design and EPC qualifications across mechanical, municipal, petrochemical and power sectors.

Our engineering center works on high-speed direct drive and low-speed high-torque drive machines — the two architectures behind every pump and fan we supply.

What we have delivered

  • Boiler feed — 120 t/h and 130/160 t/h units replacing multistage centrifugal feed pumps in industrial boilers, 21% and 19% energy saving.
  • Mine dewatering — three pump houses at −50, −250 and −500 m, six machines replacing 24 centrifugal units, 22–37% saving.
  • Coke dry quenching waste-heat boiler — feed pump replacement, 26.7% saving, 3.2-year payback.
  • Oilfield injection — seven-plunger station at Shengli Oilfield, specific power 0.3 kWh/m³·MPa.

How we work

Most of our projects start with a measured duty point rather than a specification. We ask for flow, pressure, hours and a power reading, then propose a machine and a number. If the saving is not there, we say so — a pump that cannot pay for itself is not a project.
