Multi-plunger pumps, 7–320 m³/h up to 70 MPa

Three, five and seven-plunger machines with mechanical crank drive. Stable efficiency above 90%, wear parts from 5,000 hours, and a footprint that replaces a multistage centrifugal pump without re-piping the station.

Why reciprocating instead of centrifugal

Above roughly 10 MPa, a multistage centrifugal pump is fighting its own physics: every stage adds clearance losses, and efficiency falls away as wear rings open. A plunger pump displaces a fixed volume per stroke, so its volumetric efficiency stays high for the life of the plunger — typically above 90% in continuous service.

The practical consequence is energy. On a 400 m³/h duty at 20 MPa, the difference between 62% and 90% efficiency is roughly 1,100 kW of shaft power — around 8.8 million kWh a year at 8,000 hours.

Retrofit without civil works. Our pump heads are arranged with the same suction and discharge orientation as the multistage centrifugal unit, so the replacement is a piping tie-in rather than a pump-house rebuild.

Mechanical drive, not hydraulic

Most Chinese plunger pumps in industrial service are hydraulically driven: an oil power pack pressurises a cylinder that pushes the plunger. That architecture adds a hydraulic station, heat management, oil filtration and a second family of failure modes. Our machines use a crankshaft, connecting rod and crosshead — the architecture used in oilfield pumps, where availability is the whole point.

  • No hydraulic power pack, no oil cooler, no servo valve
  • Lower parasitic load, so more of the motor output reaches the fluid
  • Faults are mechanical and visible — not hidden in a hydraulic circuit

Frequently asked

Can it replace our existing multistage centrifugal pump directly?

In most cases yes. The pump head is laid out with the same suction and discharge orientation, so the baseplate and piping tie in with minor modification. We ask for a piping sketch and the existing duty point before confirming.

What maintenance does it need?

Plungers, valve seats and seals are consumables with intervals from 5,000 hours. The crank, crosshead and frame are designed for 30,000 hours between overhauls. All wear-part replacement is done on site.

Do you supply the motor and VFD?

Yes — the unit can be supplied as a bare pump, a pump-and-motor set, or a complete skid with VFD, instrumentation and controls.

Which standards apply?

The UDZ range is built to Chinese national standards for reciprocating positive-displacement pumps. Material certificates, test reports and any third-party inspection required for your project are agreed at order stage — we state clearly what is and is not certified for each market.
