How to read the result

These are screening numbers, not a selection. They assume clean-fluid power with the efficiency you enter, and they ignore NPSH, viscosity correction and motor margin. A real selection adds 10–15% motor margin and checks the suction condition.

What usually surprises people

The shaft power of a worn centrifugal pump is often 1.6–2× the new pump — not because the motor is oversized, but because efficiency has fallen away from the design point. Measured input power is the single most useful number you can give us.

Payback assumptions

Simple payback only. It ignores maintenance savings (typically 30–50% of the energy saving on abrasive duties), downtime and the residual value of the old unit — so the real payback is usually shorter than shown.
